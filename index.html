How to play

  1. Each step, a square lights up on the grid and a voice says a letter.
  2. At level 1, compare each step with the step right before it: step 2 against step 1, step 3 against step 2, and so on.
  3. If the square is in the same place, tap Position. If the letter is the same, tap Sound. Both can match at once, so tap both.
  4. At level 2, compare against two steps back: step 3 against step 1, step 4 against step 2. Level n means n steps back.
  5. A round has 20 + n steps, 3 seconds each. After the round, if both scores are 90% or more your level goes up. If either is below 75% it goes down. Otherwise it stays.
  6. Play 12 rounds a day (≈20 minutes). Aim for 20 days. Your day resets at 4 AM.

Score per channel = hits ÷ (hits + misses + false alarms). Keyboard: A position, L sound.
